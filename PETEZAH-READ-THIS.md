Everything below was built by us on top of the base. It is **not** part of PeteZah
and is not something you host or are responsible for:

- Full user accounts — username/password login with durable sessions
- "Scrolls" — a play-money virtual economy (no real-world value, not purchasable)
- A casino hub: Crash, Blackjack, Slots, Coinflip, Dice, Roulette, Mines, Plinko,
  Wheel, Cases, and Case Battles
- Daily reward streaks, a weekly tournament, and redeemable event codes
- Cosmetics: name colors, animated name/title effects, profile banners, avatar rings
- A chat system with slash-commands, equippable cosmetics, and moderation tools
- Friends, private DMs, and unread-message notifications
- Public profile pages with stats and achievements
- Block & report tools
- An admin panel for moderation and events
- Installable "Add to Home Screen" (PWA) support

## How we try to stay within your rules

We read your Terms of Service (version 2026-09-16). Here is our good-faith effort to
respect it:

- **Non-commercial.** ScrollHub is free. We don't charge, sell, resell, or profit
  from your work or the games. (Your ToS §3(i) — no commercial exploitation.)
- **No ownership claims.** We do not claim to own your code or the games. Game IP
  belongs to the respective studios; the base code belongs to PeteZah and its
  upstream open-source projects. (§6, §9.)
- **Open-source compliance.** Our full source is public, and we keep the original
  license and attribution notices for any open-source components (including any
  AGPL-licensed parts, per your §9.4):
  - Source: https://github.com/scrollreader11-dev/scroll-backend
- **DMCA / takedowns honored.** We keep a DMCA/contact process and will disable
  anything under our control on a valid request. (§6.6.)
- **No circumvention help.** We do not build tools to defeat school/network filters,
  and we don't encourage breaking acceptable-use policies. (§1.2, §3.)
- **We'll stop on request.** If you want us to stop proxying your servers, change our
  setup, or take ScrollHub down entirely, just email us and we will.
